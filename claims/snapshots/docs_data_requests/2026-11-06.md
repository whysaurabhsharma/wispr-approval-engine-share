Data requests: get a copy of your data or ask us to delete it

                Last updated 2 days ago

Available on:
 Mac, Windows, iOS and Android.

Get a copy of your data
Request an export in the Mac or Windows app; on iPhone and Android, contact support to request a copy. We build a single archive you download while signed in. We do not email the archive, but we email you whenever an export is requested on your account.
Timing:
 most archives are quick; a large account can take a few hours.
Availability:
 the download stays available for 30 days, then expires and you request a new one.
One at a time:
 cancel the current request to start again, and expect a short wait between requests.
Important:
 Anyone signed in to your account can request an export. If you receive an export email you did not request, cancel the request and secure your account.
Delete your account and its data
This removes your account along with your dictation history, audio, meeting content, notes, dictionary, preferences and usage history, and revokes connected integrations. We also instruct the providers that process data on our behalf to delete it.
Warning:
 Deleting your account cancels an active Wispr-billed subscription immediately, with no refund, not even a prorated one. Contact support before you delete if you want a refund.
Before you start
App Store and Google Play billing:
 deleting your Wispr account does not cancel those subscriptions. Cancel with Apple or Google, or billing continues.
Local history:
 history on your other devices is not removed — delete the app on each device.
Want a copy first:
 request the export and download it before you delete.

Delete your data but keep your account
On Mac and Windows, you can delete your data while your account, sign-in, settings and subscription stay active. On iPhone and Android, contact support instead.
Warning:
 Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. Request and download your export first if you may want a copy of anything you are deleting.
Open
 Settings, then Data & Privacy.
Select
 Delete your data.
Type
 DELETE to confirm, then start the deletion.
The copy on the device you used is removed straight away; the row shows the completion date when the run finishes. You cannot start another deletion while one is running, and after several requests in quick succession you may be asked to wait before trying again. Deletion covers your data up to the time of your request, not anything you create afterwards.
Timing and what we keep
Deletion may take up to thirty days to complete. Some records stay afterwards, either because we are required to keep them or because they do not belong to your account alone:
Billing and invoice records.
A record of your data request, kept as proof that we acted on it.
Signed agreements, such as a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement.
Organization records and shared support accounts, if you used Flow through an organization.
If you use Flow through work
If your organization provided your Flow account, your organization controls that data. Send your request to your admin or IT team; they contact us and we act on their instruction. If you contact us directly, we acknowledge your request and refer it to your organization.
Deleting a work account can remove your seat and affect your organization's subscription, so it is usually not the right route.
