Manage data sharing, cloud storage and local history

                Last updated 15 hours ago

Available on:
 Mac, Windows, iOS and Android. Agreement actions differ by platform.
Flow keeps model improvement and dictation cloud storage separate: changing one does not change the other. Check each control independently.

Control
What it changes
Improve the model for everyone
Allows or blocks use of your Notetaker and dictation content to improve Wispr's models.
Dictation cloud storage
Stores dictation transcripts, audio and history on Wispr's servers for cross-device access.
Both switches default to on unless restricted and save immediately in either direction, without confirmation; 
Dictation cloud storage
 appears when available for your account. Wispr does not sell your data, and aggregate usage statistics such as words dictated do not include dictation contents.

Important:
 For confidential or privileged content, turn 
Improve the model for everyone
 off before recording. Turning it off does not stop cloud processing. For model improvement and evaluation, audio, transcripts, edits and other personal content are withheld; Flow still uploads dictation details such as word count, duration, app, URL, timestamp, app version, microphone, detected language and edit statistics. A dictation you choose to report is retained by Wispr and can be used to train and evaluate its models.
Dictation cloud storage
 does not control meeting storage: Notetaker transcripts and summaries are stored in Wispr's cloud, with no setting to opt out. On desktop, turning cloud storage off does not stop recording once Flow confirms your organization and agreement details, but blocks new recordings while it is still confirming them or cannot confirm your preferences.
When a setting is locked
On desktop, check a disabled control's tooltip for its cause. You cannot override the restriction in the app; other unlocked changes still save.
A signed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) locks sharing off. A personal or organization BAA locks 
Dictation cloud storage
 off, even if another organization setting permits sharing. Notetaker works alongside a signed personal or organization BAA, so you do not need to revoke your BAA to record meetings. Organization sharing and cloud-storage policies are independent: sharing can be locked off while cloud storage remains individually controlled. On Enterprise, sharing stays off, so Wispr does not use these organizations' dictation content to improve its models.

Check that changes have applied
Sharing and cloud-storage choices follow your account across devices, and opening desktop Settings refreshes them. For active business and enterprise subscriptions, opening Data and Privacy or returning focus to its window refreshes organization policy without restarting Flow. Check the displayed state after a policy change rather than assuming an earlier choice returns.
On desktop, organization restrictions remain in effect during connection errors; Flow removes the saved organization policy only after confirming you no longer belong to an organization. Organization policy changes still apply on launch or sign-in. Switching accounts never deletes the history of the account you sign in to under the previous account's policy.
Signing out resets the cloud-storage preference to on and clears the previous organization for the next sign-in; review both settings afterward. A signed BAA remains in effect and keeps cloud storage locked off. Signing in again does not reset your other preferences, and on desktop a required sign-in after session expiry does not reset cloud storage. Both sign-out and a required sign-in clear the device's recorded signature details, not the BAA itself.
If Flow cannot confirm a sharing preference, agreement or organization policy, it does not share dictation audio; your device's opt-out holds.

A setting is locked with no explanation, or your change does not save: contact support with your platform, plan, device, the control's exact message and what you tried.
