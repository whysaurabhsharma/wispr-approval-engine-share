When these settings are locked for you
A signed BAA locks both controls off, and organization policies can lock them too; check each control's explanation. An organization model-improvement restriction alone does not mean cloud storage is off — check it separately.

Free and Pro plans are not HIPAA-ready, and personal BAAs cannot be signed from the app. Growth includes HIPAA readiness with a signed BAA, covering dictation and Notetaker-compatible use.

What a signed BAA changes
AI text features:
 Polish, Transforms, instruct mode and Flow's other AI text features work under a BAA, and their text is only ever processed by AI providers covered by Wispr's BAA.
Notetaker:
 Teams and individuals under a signed BAA can use Notetaker on Mac and Windows. Recording, syncing and the Notetaker hub work, with privacy and organization settings still respected, and an organization can keep its BAA signed with Notetaker switched on. Some features, such as pre-meeting briefs, don't run under a BAA. Your plan, subscription or an admin disabling Notetaker can still block it for reasons unrelated to HIPAA.
Notes (iOS):
 Accounts with a BAA, or with 
Improve the model for everyone
 turned off, can still use note cloud sync, AI summaries, manual sync and Spotlight/Siri note features; sync requires being signed in and online.
Changing BAA coverage
Important:
 Review 
How to Revoke a HIPAA/BAA Agreement
 or contact support before changing agreement coverage.
Revoking removes the agreement's settings locks without changing your stored model-improvement or cloud-storage choices. Settings left off stay off; if cloud storage was already on but blocked by the BAA, syncing can resume immediately unless another policy blocks it.
Existing personal BAA:
 On Mac or Windows, the HIPAA row offers View signed BAA and Revoke BAA. Revoking is permanent — a personal BAA cannot be signed again.
Organization BAA:
 Admins whose organization BAA is recorded for their account use Open Admin Portal to manage revocation for the team; members contact their admin.
Personal BAA signed before joining an organization:
 Only that member can revoke it — an admin revoking the organization BAA cannot clear it, and the remaining personal BAA can continue restricting the member.
Revocation retains the agreement record. On desktop, successful revocation immediately removes the downloadable copy; a failed request, or signing in or out during it, leaves protections unchanged.
Still need help?
Notetaker still blocked under a BAA:
 Update Wispr Flow on Mac or Windows to the latest version and try again; if recording stays blocked, contact support with your organization and platform.
Missing download button or download still failing:
 Contact support with your platform, account type and what you tried.
Compliance or plan questions:
 Contact your account representative about your organization's use, disabled portal controls, plan requirements or compliance documentation under NDA.
