HIPAA Compliance & Healthcare Use

                Last updated 11 hours ago

Available on:
 Mac, Windows, iOS, Android. BAA signing is available on Mac, Windows and iOS; downloading an individual signed copy requires Mac or Windows. Organization controls depend on your plan and admin permissions.

When these settings are locked for you
A signed BAA locks both controls off, and organization policies can lock them too; check each control's explanation. An organization model-improvement restriction alone does not mean cloud storage is off — check it separately.

Warning:
 Do not include PHI in support feedback. Typed in-app feedback is a deliberate support submission and is not stripped by these settings or a BAA; diagnostic logs or accessibility reports may be attached, and general feedback logs are not filtered.

Obtain your signed agreement
Wispr does not countersign BAAs. The BAA is a click-through agreement; the signed copy with its Signature Record is the documentation to provide. MSA redlines or custom signed documentation require our highest-tier Enterprise plan and go through your sales representative.

What a signed BAA changes
AI text features:
 Polish, Transforms, instruct mode and Flow's other AI text features work under a BAA, and their text is only ever processed by AI providers covered by Wispr's BAA.
Notetaker:
 An organization can keep a signed BAA and Notetaker switched on at the same time, but recording meetings under HIPAA is not supported: accounts covered by HIPAA cannot use Notetaker. Your plan, subscription or an admin disabling Notetaker can block it for reasons unrelated to HIPAA.
Notes (iOS):
 Accounts with a BAA, or with 
Improve the model for everyone
 turned off, can still use note cloud sync, AI summaries, manual sync and Spotlight/Siri note features; sync requires being signed in and online.
Changing BAA coverage
Important:
 Review 
How to Revoke a HIPAA/BAA Agreement
 or contact support before changing agreement coverage.
Revoking removes the agreement's settings locks without changing your stored model-improvement or cloud-storage choices. Settings left off stay off; if cloud storage was already on but blocked by the BAA, syncing can resume immediately unless another policy blocks it.
Personal BAA:
 The personal BAA dialog can offer View signed BAA and Revoke BAA.
Organization BAA:
 Admins whose organization BAA is recorded for their account use Open Admin Portal to manage revocation for the team; members contact their admin.
Personal BAA signed before joining an organization:
 Only that member can revoke it — an admin revoking the organization BAA cannot clear it, and the remaining personal BAA can continue restricting the member.
Revocation retains the agreement record, and signing again creates a new agreement. On desktop, successful revocation immediately removes the downloadable copy; a failed request, or signing in or out during it, leaves protections unchanged.
