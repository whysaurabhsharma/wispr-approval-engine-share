FAQs
Can I get a Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA)?
Under the Standard Contractual Clauses framework, the data controller — your organization — completes the TIA. Wispr provides supporting materials through the Trust Center: the DPA with the EU SCCs and Annex III, the SOC 2 Type II report, and factual detail on our security controls and data minimization.
Is a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) available?
Yes — for healthcare customers on the Growth and Enterprise plans, and Flow is HIPAA-ready with a signed BAA. An organization admin signs it in the admin portal; the org-wide HIPAA row is disabled on other plans and for non-admins, marked “Available on Growth and Enterprise”. If you previously signed a personal BAA, your desktop app still shows “HIPAA enabled”, where you can download or permanently revoke it — you cannot sign a personal BAA again.
