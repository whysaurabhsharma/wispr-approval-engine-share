How to Revoke a HIPAA/BAA Agreement

                Last updated 1 day ago

Available on:
 Mac, Windows. For personal agreements: A BAA can be signed on iOS, but it can only be revoked from the desktop app.
No longer need your HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Wispr Flow? For an eligible personal account, revoke it in desktop settings and optionally save a signed copy first.
How to revoke your BAA
These steps apply to individual (personal) BAAs, including a personal BAA signed before joining an enterprise. Your current subscription—not organization membership alone—determines the individual or Org-wide HIPAA layout: without an active Business/Enterprise subscription, you see individual controls. Organizations with live billing require admin management instead; self-serve signing and revoking are blocked even if individual buttons appear.
BAA status belongs to your account, not your device, so use the same account on desktop if you signed on iOS. On iOS, a signed BAA locks

 Revoking has no confirmation prompt and takes effect immediately in this desktop app, even if account-status updates are delayed. Sign a new BAA before resuming workflows that require one.

If the message blocking Dictation cloud storage offers a revoke action, you can use it instead. It uses the same success and error messages as Settings and closes after a successful revoke.
How to download your signed BAA
Signed-copy downloads are for individual (non-enterprise) users. If offered in your settings, Download signed BAA appears once a signer name is recorded, which can take up to a minute after signing.
The button shows a loading state and is disabled while preparing HIPAA-BAA-signed.pdf. The PDF contains the agreement and a Signature Record page showing Signed by, Email and Date; that page is for reference, not a legal certificate.
“Couldn't download the signed agreement” means no partial file was saved. Signing out or switching accounts during a download also discards the file.

If you see “Error revoking HIPAA BAA”
The revoke did not take effect; this error also appears if no active BAA remains. The button stays available, but rapid repeated signing or revoking attempts can be temporarily blocked.
Wait
 briefly before clicking Revoke BAA again.
If the success message appears, you're done; otherwise, contact support.
If only one HIPAA button appears
The single-button layout shows HIPAA enabled → Revoke BAA or Enable HIPAA → View and accept. It has no download, Org-wide HIPAA row or admin-portal buttons; enterprise billing restrictions still apply.
What changes after revoking

Dictation cloud storage:
 Where available, this setting is locked off by a personal or org BAA: “Dictation Cloud Storage is locked off because HIPAA BAA is signed.” An organization storage-policy lock instead shows “This setting is managed by your organization.”
Improve the model for everyone:
 A personal BAA locks sharing off: “Your HIPAA agreement does not allow data to be used for model training.” With an org-only BAA, organization policy controls sharing; a policy lock shows “Your organization does not allow data to be used for model training.”
Notetaker works alongside a signed personal or organization BAA, so you do not need to revoke your BAA to record meetings.
Where Scratch Pad sync depends on Dictation cloud storage, turning storage off stops notes syncing across desktop and mobile; the sync button shows “Turn on Dictation Cloud Storage to sync notes.”
Revoking marks your BAA as revoked rather than deleting it. BAA records are not part of your data export and remain retained after usage-data deletion or account deletion.
Enterprise accounts
If you see Org-wide HIPAA, the controls depend on your role. Neither enterprise row offers Download signed BAA; contact your admin or use the portal for agreement records.

 Open admin portal opens

An org BAA keeps storage locked after a personal revoke; only an eligible admin can revoke it. Organization policies may still restrict settings afterward. When organization agreements take precedence, a personal BAA may be treated as unsigned; personal revocation does not remove organization restrictions.
Organization policy can also restrict Local data storage, showing “This setting is managed by your organization.” or “Some options are restricted by your organization.”
