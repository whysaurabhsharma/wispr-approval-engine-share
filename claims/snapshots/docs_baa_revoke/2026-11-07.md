How to Revoke a HIPAA/BAA Agreement

                Last updated 15 hours ago

Available on:
 Mac, Windows. A personal BAA can be signed on iOS, but it can only be revoked from the desktop app.
No longer need your HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Wispr Flow? For an eligible personal account, revoke it in desktop settings and optionally save a signed copy first.
How to revoke your BAA
These steps apply to individual (personal) BAAs, including one signed before joining an enterprise. Your current subscription—not organization membership alone—determines whether you see individual controls or the Org-wide HIPAA row: without an active Business/Enterprise subscription, you see individual controls. Organizations with live billing require admin management instead, so self-serve signing and revoking are blocked even if individual buttons appear.
BAA status belongs to your account, not your device, so sign in with the same account on desktop if you signed on iOS.
Warning:
 Revoking has no confirmation prompt and takes effect immediately in this desktop app, even if account-status updates are delayed. Sign a new BAA before resuming workflows that require one.
Open
 the Wispr Flow desktop app on Mac or Windows.
Go to
 Settings → Data and Privacy.
Download
 a copy first, if you want one, using Download signed BAA when available.
Click
 Revoke BAA beside HIPAA enabled.
Success shows “HIPAA BAA revoked successfully”, and the personal row changes to Enable HIPAA with View and accept. If the message blocking Dictation cloud storage offers a revoke action, you can use that instead; it shows the same messages and closes after a successful revoke.
How to download your signed BAA
Signed-copy downloads are for individual (non-enterprise) users. Download signed BAA appears once a signer name is recorded, which can take up to a minute after signing, and is disabled while the PDF is prepared. The PDF contains the agreement and a Signature Record page showing Signed by, Email and Date; that page is for reference, not a legal certificate.
“Couldn't download the signed agreement” means no partial file was saved. Signing out or switching accounts during a download also discards the file.
Check
 your network connection.
Sign
 back into the original account if you signed out or switched accounts.
Retry
 Download signed BAA; a saved PDF confirms success.
If you see “Error revoking HIPAA BAA”
The revoke did not take effect; this error also appears if no active BAA remains. The button stays available, but rapid repeated signing or revoking attempts can be temporarily blocked.
Wait
 briefly, then click Revoke BAA again. The success message means you're done; otherwise contact support.
If only one HIPAA button appears
The single-button layout shows HIPAA enabled → Revoke BAA, or Enable HIPAA → View and accept. It has no download, Org-wide HIPAA row or admin-portal buttons; enterprise billing restrictions still apply.
What changes after revoking
Signing a personal BAA turns 
Improve the model for everyone
 and Dictation cloud storage off for your account. Revoking removes personal-BAA enforcement but does not restore those settings: storage and sharing stay off until you turn them back on.
Dictation cloud storage:
 Where available, a personal or org BAA locks this setting off with “Dictation Cloud Storage is locked off because HIPAA BAA is signed.”; an organization storage-policy lock shows “This setting is managed by your organization.” instead.
Improve the model for everyone:
 A personal BAA locks sharing off with “Your HIPAA agreement does not allow data to be used for model training.” With an org-only BAA, organization policy controls sharing and a policy lock shows “Your organization does not allow data to be used for model training.”
Scratch Pad sync:
 Where sync depends on Dictation cloud storage, leaving storage off stops notes syncing across desktop and mobile, and the sync button shows “Turn on Dictation Cloud Storage to sync notes.”
Revoking marks your BAA as revoked rather than deleting it. BAA records are not part of your data export and are retained after usage-data deletion or account deletion.
Enterprise accounts
If you see Org-wide HIPAA, the controls depend on your role. Neither enterprise row offers Download signed BAA; contact your admin or use the portal for agreement records.
Admins:
 Manage the org BAA through the 
Admin Portal
, not the desktop app. When signed, View signed BAA opens the agreement externally alongside the Admin Portal button; when unsigned, only Admin Portal appears. Signing requires an active enterprise subscription and a real legal name, and both signing and revoking depend on eligibility, so not every admin can manage a BAA.
Non-admin members:
 Open admin portal opens 
the portal
 and is the only button. Members cannot revoke the org BAA or download or view the signed agreement from this row.
An org BAA keeps storage locked after a personal revoke, and only an eligible admin can revoke it. Where organization agreements take precedence, a personal BAA may be treated as unsigned, and personal revocation does not remove organization restrictions.
Sign again after revoking
Click
 View and accept beside Enable HIPAA in Settings → Data and Privacy.
Open
 the “HIPAA Business Associate Agreement.” link to read the full agreement in your browser.
Enter
 your legal name, which must contain at least one real character and be 200 characters or fewer; a blank or spaces-only name is rejected and nothing is saved. Surrounding spaces are trimmed, so “ Jane ” is stored as “Jane”.
Click
 I agree. Success shows “HIPAA BAA signed successfully”; failure shows “Error signing HIPAA BAA”.
Note:
 The dialog says “This action cannot be undone”, but Revoke BAA remains available after signing.
