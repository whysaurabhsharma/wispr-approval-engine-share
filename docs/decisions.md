## 2026-11-06: Phase 3 brief gate redefined
Old gate: briefs pass reviewer-it with no high findings on 27 of 30.
New gate: 0 unsupported statements from the faithfulness judge on every brief, and every reviewer-it high finding resolved or explicitly accepted by a human before the brief is marked reviewed.
Why: reviewer-it is a deliberately sceptical persona and raises presentation points as high on every draft; the old gate measured the reviewer's temperament, not the brief's accuracy. The new gate matches the standard used for the approval pack and consent memo.
